Pass the Story Forward

A gift from one Mayflower family member to another

You have already carried your family's Mayflower story further than anyone before you, all the way to a certified, permanent place in the Society's records. Your child or grandchild may not think much about where they came from today; most people don't, until they do. Whenever that day comes, next year or thirty years from now, the record will already be there, waiting for them.

How It Works

Because your own lineage is already proven and on file, gifting membership to your child or grandchild is far simpler than a standard application. The Historian only needs to document the most recent generation connecting you to them, not retrace your entire line back to the Mayflower. You cover the one-time application fee to begin the process; an official Membership Certificate becomes part of your family's permanent record, something to hand down and grow into over the years. Whether they continue as an annual member afterward is entirely their own choice.

You spent years doing the proving. Those years are part of you, and they're part of this gift too.

Ready to give this gift? Let us know below, and our Historian will follow up directly to help get things started.

Thank You

Our Historian will be in touch directly to help get things started. If you don't hear from us within a few days, feel free to reach out at SMDAL.Info@gmail.com.
