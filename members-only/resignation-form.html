Membership Resignation Form

We DO NOT want to lose you, but if you must resign, please use this form

← Back to SMDAL Top 12 List

Your Lineage-Certified Annual Dues-Paying Membership in the Society of Mayflower Descendants in the State of Alabama (SMDAL) will remain your financial responsibility unless and until you formally submit a signed resignation. Your Lineage-Certified Life Membership will continue until we receive notice of your death or you formally submit a signed resignation.

Fill out this form, then electronically sign it and press the SUBMIT button below. You will not be able to submit the form until you have electronically signed it. No matter which form you use, we will reach out to you to verify that it was indeed you who submitted the resignation, so please be expecting our communication via email and/or phone call and/or text message. Your resignation will not be processed until we are able to verify its authenticity with you.

Prefer to mail in a paper form instead? Download and print the PDF Resignation Form, fill it out, sign it, and mail it to SMDAL, 3500 Fowl River RD, Theodore, AL 36582-7604.
