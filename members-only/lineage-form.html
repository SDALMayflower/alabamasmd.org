Supplemental Lineage Review Form

For current members submitting an additional (supplemental) Mayflower lineage

← Back to Members Only menu

Use this form to submit a supplemental lineage for review by the Historian. This is separate from the Preliminary Lineage Review used by first-time applicants — it's only for members who already belong to SMDAL and are documenting an additional Mayflower ancestor.

Before You Begin
If the supplemental lineage you are submitting turns out to be valid, do you intend to pay the "Supplemental Fee for Additional Lineages" and have the Historian submit it to GSMD for certification? *

You can pay the fee at PaySMDAL.today, but please do not do so until the Historian notifies you of the validity of your proposed supplemental lineage.
