Tennessee Valley Colony Luncheon & Featured Speaker

Saturday, May 23, 2026 • Huntsville, Alabama

← Back to Events

Saturday, May 23, 2026 • Valley Hills Country Club • Huntsville, Alabama

There were 22 attendees including members, children, and 1 prospective guest.

During lunch, attendees met and visited with their Mayflower cousins in groups.

During the meeting, Sarah Curtis of Madison County, Alabama, was nominated, elected and installed as the Colony's new Lt. Governor.

After lunch, Kay Anderson, historical costume designer and seamstress, presented an interesting program and displays on Mayflower Pilgrim attire.

After the program, everyone continued visiting with one another and enjoying their time together.
