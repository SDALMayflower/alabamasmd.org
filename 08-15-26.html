Capital Colony Governors Luncheon

Saturday, August 15, 2026 • Pike Road, Alabama

← Back to Events

Saturday, August 15, 2026 • Cucos Mexican Cafe • Pike Road, Alabama

Four SMDAL Governors gathered for the Capital Colony Governors Luncheon: Former Governor Rich Hobson, Former Governor Mary Helen Mahan, Former Governor and Deputy Governor General Alan Davis, and current Governor Kevin Sellew.

After lunch, Governor Sellew presented information about several initiatives he is pursuing with the State Society and the Colonies, then unveiled the content of the new Members Only Portal on the new SMDAL website to those in attendance.

Attendees hailed from Barbour, Mobile, Montgomery, and Shelby Counties in Alabama.
