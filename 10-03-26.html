TVC 35th Anniversary Mayflower Families Reunion

Saturday, October 3, 2026 • Doors Open at 2:00 p.m. • Huntsville, Alabama

← Back to Events

Saturday, October 3, 2026
Faith Presbyterian Church USA Fellowship Hall
Huntsville, Alabama

The purpose of this event is to give the members of the Tennessee Valley Colony of Mayflower Descendants and their families an opportunity to gather and experience a unique Mayflower event. It’s just like a “normal” family reunion with games, potluck food and photos, but it celebrates the numerous Mayflower Families from whom we all descend.

All Mayflower-descended family members (plus their spouses and partners!) who are not members of the Alabama Society of Mayflower Descendants are welcome to come to this party. No meeting. Just food, games and Mayflower Families group photos.

TVC LT GOV Sarah Curtis is determined to entertain the children attending this gathering. She has purchased table displays of educational and interactive materials for children, e.g. “Build the Mayflower,” maps to label the voyage, Pilgrim costumes, games of Pilgrim children, plus information about Thanksgiving foods, and numerous children’s books.

She has also planned generational games of Mayflower Bingo, Pilgrim Riddles and several other activities.

To RSVP for the event, send an email to SMDAL.Info+2026-10-03-Headcount@gmail.com listing the names of attendees. Put the word “adult” next to the name of those 18 and over and the age of anyone under 18 next to their name.

To sign up to bring food for the Potluck, click or tap THIS LINK.

To invite your Mayflower-descended family members to the party, send them to this URL: AlabamaSMD.org/10-03-26.html
