TVC 35th Anniversary Mayflower Families Reunion

Saturday, October 3, 2026 • Huntsville, Alabama

← Back to Events

Saturday, October 3, 2026 • Faith Presbyterian Church USA Fellowship Hall • Huntsville, Alabama

On Saturday, October 3, 2026, TVC members and guests gathered at Faith Presbyterian Church Fellowship Hall in Huntsville for the Colony’s first Mayflower Families Reunion. About two dozen people attended, and the event also marked the 35th Anniversary of the founding of the Tennessee Valley Colony.

The purpose of this event was to give the members of the Tennessee Valley Colony of Mayflower Descendants and their families an opportunity to gather and experience a uniquely social Mayflower event. Just like a “normal” family reunion with games, potluck food and photos, but it also celebrates the numerous Mayflower Families from whom we all descend.

All Mayflower-descended family members (plus their spouses and partners!) who are not members of the Alabama Society of Mayflower Descendants were welcome to come to this party. No meeting. Just food, games and Mayflower Families group photos.

TVC LT GOV Sarah Curtis was determined to entertain the children attending this gathering. She purchased table displays of educational and interactive materials for children, e.g. “Build the Mayflower,” maps to label the voyage, Pilgrim costumes, games of Pilgrim children, plus information about Thanksgiving foods, and numerous children’s books.

She also planned generational games of Mayflower Bingo, Pilgrim Riddles and several other activities.
