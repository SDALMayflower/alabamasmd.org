"We Will Plymouth Rock You!" Weekend

The Society of Mayflower Descendants in the State of (Sweet Home) Alabama

April 24 & 25, 2026

Weekend Recap

Pictured: Tom Begley • Kevin Sellew • Brian House • Regena Dawson • Valerie Harris • Alan Davis • Jeremy Sellew • Mary Helen Mahan • Richard Lewis Hobson • Karen Peterson • Susan Field • Sandee Leonard • Mary Medbery • John Holmes Smith IV • Theresa Sellew • Leslie House • Scott Field • Dwayne Dawson • Tom Peterson • Darlene Davis • Susan Cain Hobson • Mark Fossum • Ken Mahan • Amelia Hobson Chase • Michael Duffy Chase • Susan Bayless Chase • Eva Kathryn Chase • Barrett Holt

The last weekend in April, 2026, was branded as our “We Will Plymouth Rock You!” Weekend. It began with a Friday night Welcome Dinner at T.P. Crockmier's Restaurant in downtown Mobile. Mr. Tom Begley, the Executive Director of Plimoth-Patuxet Museums in Plymouth, Massachusetts, was the honored guest. Twenty people – Members, FOTP Supporters and Guests – attended the dinner from the Capital Colony, the Gulf Coast Colony and the Tennessee Valley Colony.

Saturday's daytime schedule at the National Maritime Museum of the Gulf, which sits right on the bank of the Mobile River, began with check-in and pick-up of “Swag Bags” by all attendees, followed by the SMDAL Annual Business Meeting, then a Mayflower Cousins Luncheon at the Fresh Fruit Café inside the museum. After lunch, Mr. Tom Begley, our featured speaker for the event, gave an hour-long presentation about how “Revolutionary Ideas Started Here” – with “here” referring to what we now know as Plymouth Colony and showing how Plymouth Colony affected the eventual American Revolution.

At the conclusion of Mr. Begley's presentation, the event was adjourned and the group was given a private guided tour of the entire museum complex. It is a fascinating facility and a very apropos location due to our own seafaring ancestries.

On Saturday evening, the Gulf Coast Colony hosted its 40th Anniversary Party in the form of a Mayflower Families Reunion. There were no children present to play games with, so the event morphed into a group dinner at Moe's Original BBQ in downtown Mobile.

Gulf Coast Colony's 40th Anniversary cake, marking four decades as the oldest of SMDAL's colonies.

The NMMoG was chosen as the conference location, in keeping with the newly adopted theme of having SMDAL Spring Conferences at “destination venues” around Alabama. Attendees were also encouraged to build a vacation week or weekend around our event. Some folks came early, some folks stayed an extra day or two and some did both, spending their time touring attractions in and around Mobile County and visiting with family and friends who live in the area.

Weekend Stats

  • Attendees hailed from Baldwin, Madison, Mobile, and Montgomery Counties, representing the Capital, Gulf Coast, and Tennessee Valley Colonies.
  • Thirteen SMDAL Members were in attendance, nine of whom held elected or appointed office.
  • Seven 2026 FOTP Supporters attended.
  • There were eight non-member Guests in attendance (including the featured speaker).
  • Eight of the twenty BoA Members were in attendance.

If any attendees have additional photos from any part of the Weekend Event that are not shown on this page, please send them (with caption) to SMDAL.Info@gmail.com so they can be included on this page celebrating the event.

In Loving Memory

Alethea Wallace Porter Cole-Tyson

November 23, 1941 – March 10, 2026

The Society paused during its Annual Business Meeting to remember Alethea, a life member and descendant of Mayflower passenger William Bradford, whose warmth and steady encouragement will be missed by all who knew her.

Thank you to everyone who attended the “We Will Plymouth Rock You! Weekend” in Mobile. It was a memorable gathering for the Alabama Society of Mayflower Descendants, and we look forward to seeing everyone at future events.
