Membership

Honor Your Ancestors, Preserve Their Legacy

Eligibility

Membership is open to any person able to document their direct lineage from one or more of the passengers on the Mayflower voyage that terminated at Plymouth, New England, in December 1620.

The Application Process

Our State Historian and local colonies are here to assist you in navigating the genealogical requirements. The process begins with a preliminary review of your lineage documentation to ensure all standards of the General Society of Mayflower Descendants are met.

Lineage Submission Form

This is a Synopsis of the Lineage of an applicant seeking membership in The Society of Mayflower Descendants in the State of Alabama.

Please fill in the line carrier spaces from you to the Mayflower passenger (including spouses), for each generation, wherever possible. The spouses of the direct lineage from your Mayflower ancestor are always placed in the right column after the word "Married." The final name cited should be your own name along with that of your spouse if you have been or are currently married.

Let us know if there has been any blood relative who has been a member of the Mayflower Society, in any state, (with state and state number & General Society number if known). If available please include a copy of the member's application and inform us at the end of this form that you are providing this additional information. Any other information that you cannot fit into the rest of the form can be provided here as well.

Fill out starting with the name of your Pilgrim ancestor. On the next line, enter his/her son or daughter, and who they married. Then list the next son or daughter and their spouse. Continue through the generations down to yourself.

We will compare your proposed lineage with the lineage papers in our files to determine what portion of your line may already be documented. You will receive an email message on the findings with instructions on how to proceed.
