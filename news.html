The New AlabamaSMD.org Is Here: Members Portal Now Open

The Society of Mayflower Descendants in the State of (Sweet Home) Alabama

Announcement Posted August 22, 2026

We're proud to officially unveil the new Alabama Society website, live now at AlabamaSMD.org.

The centerpiece of this launch is the new secure Members Portal, built specifically to orient both new and long-time members. Inside you'll find the SMDAL Top 12 List (covering everything from what we need from you, to member benefits, to preparing your estate, to national, state, and colony events), an online Supplemental Lineage Review Form, the current Membership Directory, and the SMDAL Bylaws, with three more sections still under construction.

Current members: check your email for portal access details.

We've also recently refreshed the public Membership and Events pages. Take a look around.

New Feature Posted September 2026

Growing the SMDAL Toolbox: Research On-Ramp & Passenger Roster

We're steadily building out a toolbox of free resources for members and prospective members working on their lineage. The two newest additions: the Research On-Ramp, with a passenger checker tool to confirm whether a Mayflower passenger has documented descendants, and a full Passenger & Spouse Roster covering all 26 GSMD-documented passenger families with the spouse married to each one. Names only, sourced and dated, with links straight to GSMD's own family profiles for anyone who wants the full story. More tools are on the way.
