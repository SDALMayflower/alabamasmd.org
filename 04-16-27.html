Martin W. “Marty” Beerman

GSMD Governor General (2026–2029)

Martin W. “Marty” Beerman is currently serving GSMD as Governor General. From 2023-2026 he served as Secretary General, Meeting and Events Committee Chair, New Product Committee Chair, Bylaws Committee, Long Term Planning Committee, Personnel Committee, and Technology Committee.

In addition, Beerman has been active in the Nebraska Society since joining in 1990, including six years as their newsletter editor and the last twenty years as webmaster. He served as Historian from 2007–2012 and 2015-2025, plus serving as Nebraska Society Governor from 2017–2020. He has also spent many years chairing the Nebraska Society Scholarship program. At the 2019 General Board of Assistants Meeting, he was recognized with a Certificate of Commendation and Appreciation. That certificate noted that as Historian, he was responsible for a record number of new applications processed, launching the Nebraska Society website, and maintaining their membership database.

Beerman participates in many genealogy and lineage organizations and has joined over forty significant societies, far too numerous to list. He is currently serving as the President of The Warren Cousins Family Society.

Beerman retired in 2021 from a career in healthcare marketing. His Master’s Degree in Healthcare Administration was from Bellevue University. Beerman spent nearly forty years working with hospitals in the Omaha area. From 2007 until his retirement in 2021, he served as Vice President of Marketing and Community Relations for Children’s Hospital and Medical Center in Omaha. During that interval, he somehow found time to serve on Boards of Directors for the American Heart Association, the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society, the Omaha Theater for Young People, the Omaha Children’s Museum, and several other organizations devoted to children’s health and development.

Our Society welcomes the Governor General to the State of Sweet Home Alabama. During his time with us, he will install the members of our next BoA for the 2027-2030 term.
