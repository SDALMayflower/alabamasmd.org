Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

This page is still growing — here's what we can answer today, with more on the way. If you don't see your question below, reach out through our Contact form and we'll get back to you directly.

How do I become a member?

Membership is open to those who can document direct descent from a passenger of the Mayflower's 1620 voyage to Plymouth. Visit our Membership page for an overview, or head straight to the Application Process page for a step-by-step guide.

How do I research or verify my Mayflower lineage?

Not sure where your line connects? Submit a Preliminary Lineage Review through the form on our Membership page, and we'll let you know what's needed. Our Useful Links page also has a curated list of genealogy research resources, including Ancestry, FamilySearch, and WikiTree.

How do I contact the Society?

Use our Contact form to send a message directly to our officers, or email us at SMDAL.Info@gmail.com.

What are the local colonies?

The Alabama Society is organized into four local colonies: Cahaba River Colony (Birmingham Metro area), Capital Colony (Montgomery Metro area), Gulf Coast Colony (Mobile Bay Metro area), and Tennessee Valley Colony (Huntsville Metro area).

Where can I find upcoming events?

Visit our Events page for our conference, meetings, and other upcoming gatherings.
