Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

This page is still growing — here's what we can answer today, with more on the way. If you don't see your question below, reach out through our Contact form and we'll get back to you directly.

How do I research or verify my Mayflower lineage?

If you are exploring a family tradition or learning how to bridge your lines backward, visit our Research On-Ramp. It provides a step-by-step guide on how to work from yourself backward one generation at a time and distinguish research clues from primary evidence.

If you already have your lineage documented back through your middle generations, you can submit a Preliminary Lineage Review directly on our Membership page. Our Useful Links page also has a curated list of research resources.

How do I become a member?

Membership is open to those who can document direct descent from a passenger of the Mayflower's 1620 voyage to Plymouth. Visit our Membership page for an overview of eligibility, dues, benefits, and the five-step application process.

How do I contact the Society?

Use our Contact form to send a message directly to our officers, or email us directly at SMDAL.Info@gmail.com.

What are the local colonies?

The Alabama Society is organized into four local colonies: Cahaba River Colony (Birmingham Metro area), Capital Colony (Montgomery Metro area), Gulf Coast Colony (Mobile Bay Metro area), and Tennessee Valley Colony (Huntsville Metro area).

Where can I find upcoming events?

Visit our Events page for our annual Spring Conference, Compact Day commemoration, and other upcoming gatherings across Alabama.
